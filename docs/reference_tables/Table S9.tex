\parbox{\linewidth}{\raggedright\fontsize{9}{11}\selectfont\textit{Notes:} Estimates are adjusted differences in annual frailty-index (FI) change between substantial wealth recovery and persistent loss or limited recovery, expressed in FI units/year, with model-based Wald 95\% confidence intervals (CIs). Negative estimates indicate slower annual FI increase in the substantial-recovery group. Models were cohort-specific, unweighted maximum-likelihood linear mixed models with participant-specific random intercepts. Primary estimates are repeated for reference. All analyses retained the original 50\% recovery classification, FI definition and follow-up timing. Sample restrictions were applied at the participant level, retaining all eligible model observations for included participants. The absolute-loss restriction used the 25th percentile among all participants with a first observed qualifying shock in each cohort; thresholds are detailed in Supplementary Methods S2.2. The log-transformed analysis replaced the wealth-loss proportion and its time interaction with standardised $\log(1+\text{loss proportion})$ terms. The equivalisation restriction required the same factor at the pre-shock, shock and landmark interviews. \textsuperscript{\ensuremath{\dagger}}In KLoSA, this factor was always one, so the sample and estimates equal the primary analysis and the model was not refitted. The positive-wealth restriction was a supplementary stress test. Numerical sensitivity involving loss-related adjustment terms warrants caution for the ELSA lowest-quintile exclusion and the HRS and ELSA positive-wealth analyses. Restricted samples represent different populations and do not constitute measurement-error correction. HRS, Health and Retirement Study; ELSA, English Longitudinal Study of Ageing; KLoSA, Korean Longitudinal Study of Aging.}
