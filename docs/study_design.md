# Study design

1. Identify eligible consecutive interviews in harmonized HRS, ELSA and KLoSA. At t − 1 require age ≥60, positive wealth, and valid wealth at t − 1 and t.
2. Select the first observed qualifying shock within each cohort's locked search window, before checking recovery or later FI availability. A later shock never replaces a first shock with missing recovery/outcomes.
3. At the next scheduled interview (t + 1), classify substantial recovery as regaining ≥50% of the index-shock-related wealth loss. The reference is persistent loss or limited recovery. Recovery is not a return to 50% of pre-shock wealth.
4. The landmark FI at t + 1 and all eligible later FI assessments enter primary cohort-specific random-intercept linear mixed-effects models. Require at least one later valid FI. Time is actual elapsed years from the landmark. Prespecified covariates and their time interactions are retained.
5. Report the recovery-by-time interaction (adjusted difference in annual FI change) by cohort, with a secondary random-effects meta-analysis. Sensitivities address thresholds, measurement, weighting, missing covariates, wealth restrictions, model specification and timing.
